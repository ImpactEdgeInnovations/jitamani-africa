Brief: Bring the craft and flavours of Mumbai’s cocktail scene into Nairobi through a guest mixologist and a series of venue takeovers.

----

Role: Jitamani Africa brought Jishnu from Ekaa Mumbai to Nairobi for a three-night pop-up bar takeover, as documented in our original event account.

----

Delivery: The experience began at Hero at Trademark Hotel, followed by two nights at Sinnerman Bar at The Alchemist.

The drinks included Petrichor, inspired by the scent of rain, and a Bartender Special created for Nairobi. The programme connected a visiting mixologist’s approach with two distinct local venues.
