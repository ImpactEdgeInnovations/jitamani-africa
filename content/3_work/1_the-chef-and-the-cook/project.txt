Delivery: The programme ran from March 22 to 24, with a press briefing on March 18. Chef James Mitchell led the culinary experience; the original account credits Build X as a co-founder and Yaniv Gelnik as “The Cook.”

The menu paired experimental techniques with local ingredients, from white snapper and cured egg yolk to a dessert of baobab, pineapple, mint and peanut.
