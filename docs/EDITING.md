# Essential editing

Open `/panel`. The owner creates the first administrator locally; everyday editors should use individual **Content editor** accounts. There is no default password. Account files are private and excluded from Git.

## Website content

The **Website content** tab shows the five main pages and supporting archive/privacy pages. Select a page, update its text and save. The layout, colours and typography remain consistent. Headline accent fields provide the wine-coloured phrase; keep them short and review on a phone.

Home includes the introduction, hero photograph and caption, selected-work heading and services introduction. About contains the company introduction and two approach sections. Services has four editable rows with title, summary, audience and activities (one activity per line). Contact controls its headline and introduction.

## Photographs

Use **Shared photographs** for the homepage/About images. Upload JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF up to 10 MB. Supply a meaningful image description and photographer/source information. Select an existing image in the relevant page field or upload a replacement. The site generates responsive WebP versions automatically.

Project-specific photos live inside that project's editor. Use original high-quality files; avoid screenshots of screenshots or images copied from unapproved third parties. The renderer does not stretch a low-resolution source into a larger download. Always preview faces and important subjects at phone and desktop widths.

## Projects

Open **Our Work → Add**. Enter the project title, category/location if appropriate, short introduction and factual story. Choose a cover and optional gallery images; add captions/descriptions. Link to an archive story only when it is relevant. Save a draft until the text and image rights are approved, then publish. Order the projects in the list; the homepage uses the two most recent positions in reverse order.

Do not invent results or client relationships. A delivery narrative is sufficient when there are no verified metrics. The existing mixologist photograph belongs to the cocktail story, not a separate third project.

## Archive

Both original article URLs and publication dates are preserved. Avoid renaming them. Update article copy or images through **From the archive**. If a slug genuinely needs to change, ask the maintainer to add and test a relevant redirect first.

## Contact details

The **Contact details** tab controls displayed email, phone, WhatsApp, social links and the closing invitation. WhatsApp uses country code and digits only. Changing the displayed email does not silently change the protected SMTP delivery configuration: the maintainer must update and test the intended recipient separately.

Inquiries arrive in the business inbox after production SMTP is configured. There is no CRM to maintain. A maintainer can recover/retry a pending private record if mail delivery fails. Local tests explicitly say that no email was sent.

Before publishing, check spelling, mobile presentation, photo crops, link destinations and image descriptions. Avoid deleting a shared photograph still used by another page.


## Homepage and case-study refinements

Home includes a short company introduction with editable heading and text. Upload original photographs when available: portrait images of at least 1600px tall work best for the hero; landscape images of at least 1600px wide work best for project covers. The current hero uses a lower crop to prioritise the mixologist and his work.

Projects include optional idea, role, delivery and outcome fields. Empty sections are omitted from the public page. Only publish responsibilities and outcomes you can substantiate; no outcome figures are required. The existing Chef and The Cook article does not specify Jitamani’s exact responsibilities, so its role section remains blank.

The mobile WhatsApp link uses the contact number already stored in site settings. It hides while the contact form has focus or the navigation menu is open.


## AI platforms and event stories

Our Work has two sections. Select **AI & Digital Platform** for software projects and **Event & Experience** for event work. Existing events remain in the event section. Platform projects have a sector, title-artwork colour and live HTTPS URL; they do not require an event photograph. The platform title treatments are editorial artwork, not product screenshots or original product logos.

The owner confirmed DukaPilot, Her Africa Table and CaseReady as AI-powered platforms developed by Jitamani Africa. Their entries do not claim associated events or past AI-training engagements. Keep future results, testimonials and training case studies grounded in actual engagements.

Home has editable AI consulting/training text. Services includes AI for Business and Professional AI Training. Training links preselect the inquiry interest. The optional contact interest is retained in inquiry records and included in the email. The Railway demo continues to disable collection and displays the selected interest for context.
